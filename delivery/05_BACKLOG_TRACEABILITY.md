## Additional current business requirements

The customer requirement has two channels and two outcomes:
- Phone-originated support tickets and developer-forum questions.
- Immediate automated answering for questions that are supportable with approved evidence and automation.
- Proactive prevention of repeat calls/posts through document clarification, reusable answer strategies and, in Phase 2, understandable visual explanations.

These business outcomes extend the acceptance framing; they do not change the authoritative workbook's historical IDs or silently close its criteria.

## Do not overstate

- The r62 public site reports 57 review rows. The latest offline reproduction imports three paraphrases, with zero evaluation-eligible cases.
- The supplementary four-report version-context audit is not a validated pattern.
- The five videos are not evidence of live AI accuracy or improved comprehension.
- The strategy-evolution implementation has no real fitness run or held-out gain.
- The support sample path does not mean customer ticket data has been received or approved.
